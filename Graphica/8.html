Comprehensive Crop Insurance Schemes Portal

फसल बीमा योजना

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Flagship yield-based insurance providing comprehensive risk coverage for standing crops against natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas, including sharecroppers and tenants.

Visit official site https://pmfby.gov.in/

मौसम आधारित फसल बीमा

Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme (RWBCIS)

Protection against weather parameters like abnormal rainfall, temperature, wind, and humidity affecting sensitive crops.

Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops in notified reference units/areas.

Visit official site https://pmfby.gov.in/

राष्ट्रीय कृषि बीमा योजना

National Agricultural Insurance Scheme (NAIS)

Historical area-yield approach scheme providing extensive safety nets for food crops, oilseeds, and commercial variations.

Eligibility: All farmers growing notified food crops and oilseeds.

Visit official site https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/nais

संशोधित राष्ट्रीय कृषि बीमा योजना

Modified National Agricultural Insurance Scheme (MNAIS)

Transitional scheme featuring localized calamity assessments, actuarial premium pricing, and enhanced government subsidies.

Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops in notified implementation districts.

Visit official site https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/mnais

नारियल पादप बीमा योजना

Coconut Palm Insurance Scheme (CPIS)

Specialized protection program structured to cover coconut tree losses resulting from natural disasters like cyclones and storms.

Eligibility: All coconut growers owning healthy bearing palm trees in target regions.

Visit official site https://www.coconutboard.gov.in/

एकीकृत पैकेज बीमा योजना

Unified Package Insurance Scheme (UPIS)

Multi-risk framework designed to bundle crop insurance with life, personal accident, and farm asset covers.

Eligibility: Farmers looking for bundled insurance coverage options across assets.

Visit official site https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/upis

कृषि आय बीमा योजना

Farm Income Insurance Scheme (FIIS)

Addresses both crop yield shortages and market price drops simultaneously to guarantee minimum income thresholds.

Eligibility: Participating farmers cultivating designated crops in pilot areas.

Visit official site https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/fiis

राज्य-विशिष्ट फसल बीमा

State-Specific Custom Crop Insurance Schemes

Tailored regional programs introduced by individual states to address local agro-climatic challenges and support unique local staples.

Eligibility: Farmers residing in specific participating states under notified scheme parameters.

Visit official site https://agricoop.nic.in/

पैरामेट्रिक और व्यापक बीमा

Index-Based Comprehensive Crop Insurance

Specialized parametric solutions covering multi-peril risks such as floods, severe hailstorms, and extreme climate events.

Eligibility: Farmers opting for specialized commercial or parametric insurance covers.

Visit official site https://pmfby.gov.in/
